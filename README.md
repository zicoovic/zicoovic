## Featured Project

### Walleon — Personal Finance & Budget Tracker

A bilingual Flutter finance app for tracking expenses, managing wallets, planning budgets, and understanding spending patterns.

**Key highlights:**

- Expense and income tracking
- Wallet and transaction management
- Budget management and budget history
- Statistics and charts
- Recurring transactions
- Custom categories
- CSV export
- Arabic and English localization
- Dark mode support
- Firebase authentication and Firestore sync
- Password security and biometric app lock
- Google Play compliance links and account/data deletion flows

**Tech:** Flutter, Dart, Firebase, BLoC/Cubit, Clean Architecture, Drift, GoRouter

[View Repository](https://github.com/Walleon-app/expense_tracker_application)
